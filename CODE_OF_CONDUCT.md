# Community conduct

Be respectful, discuss evidence and code rather than personal attributes, and
make space for contributors with different experience. Harassment, threats,
discrimination and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Contact `prajwalpratapyadav5@gmail.com` privately about conduct concerns.
The maintainer may remove harmful content, ask for a change in behaviour or
restrict participation. Reports are handled discreetly within the practical
limits of a volunteer-maintained project.
