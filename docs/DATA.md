# Data, assets and historical evidence

No external datasets, model weights or personal records are needed. Bits,
noise and channel gains are generated by NumPy's seeded generator. Generated
CSV and figures inherit the repository's MIT license. Matplotlib's fonts are
provided by that dependency; no externally sourced photographs or logos appear.

The original MATLAB source, observations and PDF are retained byte for byte
under `legacy/original/`. Their claims are historical and include errors
explained in [conventions](conventions.md). The PDF has a blank roll-number field
and was reviewed for credentials and personal records. It is not an independent
measurement artifact for the corrected simulator. The original repository
already carries an MIT license; no license or authorship was replaced.
